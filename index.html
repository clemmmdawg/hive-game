HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Completely surround your opponent’s Queen Bee. The six surrounding pieces can belong to either player.

YOUR TURN

Place one new piece or move one piece already in the hive. You cannot move pieces until your Queen has been placed.

PLACING PIECES

Your first piece touches the opponent’s first piece. After that, a newly placed piece must touch your color and cannot touch the opponent’s color. Place your Queen by your fourth turn. The optional tournament opening prevents either player from leading with the Queen.

THE HIVE

Every piece must remain connected as one hive. Sliding pieces cannot squeeze through a gap blocked on both sides.
