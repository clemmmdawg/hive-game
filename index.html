HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Completely surround your opponent’s Queen Bee. The six surrounding pieces can belong to either player.

YOUR TURN

Place one new piece or move one piece already in the hive. You cannot move pieces until your Queen has been placed.

PLACING PIECES

Your first piece touches the opponent’s first piece. After that, a newly placed piece must touch your color and cannot touch the opponent’s color. Place your Queen by your fourth turn. The optional tournament opening prevents either player from leading with the Queen.

THE HIVE

Every piece must remain connected as one hive. Sliding pieces cannot squeeze through a gap blocked on both sides.

PIECE GUIDE

QUEEN
Slides one space.
BEETLE
Moves one space and can climb onto the hive.
GRASSHOPPER
Jumps in a straight line over one or more pieces.
ANT
Slides any distance around the hive.
SPIDER
Slides exactly three spaces.
MOSQUITO
Copies the movement of an adjacent piece.
LADYBUG
Moves two spaces over the hive, then one space down.
PILLBUG
Slides one space or relocates an adjacent piece.

CONTROLS

TAP
Select a piece, then tap a green hex. Amber rings mark pieces a pillbug can throw.
DRAG · PINCH
Pan and zoom the board. Scroll wheel zooms on desktop.
RECENTER
Fit the whole hive back in view.
KEYS
Esc cancel · Z undo (local) · C recenter · +/− zoom · M sound
